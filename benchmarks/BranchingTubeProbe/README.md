# Branching tube probe

Run `dotnet run --project benchmarks/BranchingTubeProbe -c Release`. The probe builds a procedural branching-tube fixture (a crossbar, a tapered stem and two tapered arms whose tips are a gap `1/8` or `1/128` apart; the union has genus 0) and closes it three ways: `MarchingCubes` on the exact hard-union field at cell sizes `1/16` and `1/32`, each on an aligned and a half-cell-shifted lattice; a chain of strict `ExactMeshBoolean` unions of 16-sided `CappedCylinderGenerator` meshes; and `MeshAlphaWrap` on the appended meshes. It prints one row per gap and route: status, components, boundary edges, bowties, Euler characteristic, genus, wall time and `MeshRobustRepair.NotCleanReason` (with the self-intersection check). No mesh files are read or written. Results and conclusions: [docs/research/BRANCHING_TUBE_GENUS_STUDY.md](../../docs/research/BRANCHING_TUBE_GENUS_STUDY.md). Times are single runs and vary between runs.
