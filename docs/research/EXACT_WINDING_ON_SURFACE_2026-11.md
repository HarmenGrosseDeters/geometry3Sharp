# Exact winding number at a point exactly on a triangle (Stage 1, 2026-11)

Status: diagnosis (Stage 1) and the decision (section 5); the change itself is in `spatial/ExactWindingCache.cs` and the tests of `ExactWindingOnSurfaceTests`. Question: what is the value of `DMeshAABBTree3.WindingNumber` / `ExactWindingCache.Evaluate` for a query point p that lies exactly on a triangle of the mesh, what was it meant to be, who depends on it, and what changes if the value is defined as that of the symbolically perturbed point.

## 1. What `tri_on_surface_correction` was meant to do

Added in one commit (f69707b2a, PR #1294, "Add exact ray-plus-boundary winding cache"; no later change). Message and code comment: "exact on-surface correction matching DMesh3.WindingNumber conventions"; comment: it replaces the own-triangle term w_T(p_eps) of the perturbed evaluation by `safe_tri_solid_angle(a, b, c, p) / 4pi` so that the result "matches the DMesh3.WindingNumber / FastWindingNumber solid-angle-sum convention on vertices (e.g. 0.125 on cube corners), edges, and coplanar faces". So it was meant to keep the value of the old floating-point solid-angle sum at on-surface points (a regression guard against the old API), not to return the integer of one side and not an exact principal value. The pinned tests compare with that sum (`ExactWindingNumberTests.OnSurfacePoints_...MatchBruteForce`, cube corner 0.125).

What the old convention really is (probe, section 3): at a vertex or an edge the floating-point sum is the principal value (B below: 0.125 at a cube corner, 0.25 on a cube edge), because the own solid angle of T there is a continuous 0 or a symmetric limit. In the interior of T, and on the diagonal shared by two coplanar triangles, the own term is +-0.5 (+-0.5 each) by the sign of a rounded zero determinant, so the sum is S + 0.5 or S - 0.5 (S = the other triangles = the mean of the two sides), that is one of the two sides chosen by rounding, not the mean. The documented value 0.5 for a face of a closed mesh ("the convention of the on-surface correction") is therefore never returned for a face interior. There is no documented contract for on-surface points: the guide says exact integers on closed meshes "for points off the surface" and, since the branch underneath, that a point on a triangle is outside the guarantee (a known open point); `IsInsideBatch` and the design note say the same.

## 2. The definitions compared

- today: `Evaluate` with the correction (`chi + boundary sum - sum of tri_on_surface_correction`).
- A: the value at the symbolically perturbed point p_eps = (x + eps3, y + eps1, z + eps2), 0 < eps3 << eps2 << eps1 (first paper, supplement 2.1). In the code this is `chi + boundary sum` WITHOUT the correction: chi already uses p_eps, and the branch cut of every boundary edge angle is coupled to chi through the shared `EdgeSignX`; `boundary_edge_angle_x` defines the limits for p on a vertex or on the open segment of a boundary edge exactly (evaluated within its bound). Nothing else is needed for the chi-plus-boundary paths: no on-surface detection at all. The soup fallback (`direct_sum`, the floating-point sum over all triangles) is the exception, section 5.
- B: the principal value, the limit of the mean of w over a small sphere around p. Because each triangle's solid angle is antisymmetric under the reflection through its plane, every triangle whose plane contains p contributes exactly 0 (p in T or not), so B = sum over the triangles with `Orient3d(a, b, c, p) != 0` of Omega_T(p) / 4pi. Face interior of a closed mesh: k + 1/2; edge: the dihedral fraction; vertex: the solid-angle fraction.

## 3. Measurement (temporary probe, not committed)

Procedural fixtures with dyadic coordinates (no libm), every point checked with `ExactPredicates.Orient3d(...) == 0` for a triangle of the mesh. Each fixture in an axis-aligned form (points on all six coordinate planes) and sheared by a dyadic matrix (generic planes, exact). Closed: cube, L prism (reflex edge), the reproducer tetrahedron, two cubes sharing an edge (non-manifold edge), two cubes glued on a face. Open: box missing one face, one triangle (in z = 0 and tilted), three pages on one edge. A soup of 72 triangles (direct-sum path). 203 points, 4 triangle orders (reversed, rotated with cyclic corners, reversed vertex ids), scales 2^-30, 2^20, 2^100. Reference of A: the cache at p + (2^-42, 2^-14, 2^-28) (an off-surface point; closed: equal to A in every row where the offset is smaller than the distance to the nearest feature; open: within 1e-4; near-edge rows with d below the offset are compared with the hemisphere rule). The reference of B is the formula above.

Counts over the rows (closed meshes, 122 points): today is a non-integer in 58, equals A in 19, equals B in 48, changes with the triangle order in 22, its `IsInsideBatch` decision differs from A in 62; A is an integer in all 122, identical across the four triangle orders and the three scales (bit for bit) in all 122, and equals the independent offset reference in all rows where the reference is valid. Open meshes (75 points): today varies with the order in 12, A in none. Both are bit-for-bit scale invariant (2^k) everywhere; the dependence of today on the order comes from the rounded determinant of the own term, and from the summation order of the boundary part.

Today minus B (the column that decides section 5): in 192 of the 197 non-soup rows it is 0, +-0.5 or +1 up to 1e-6 (80 rows 0, 101 rows +0.5, 4 rows -0.5, 7 rows +1); the other five are rows where B itself is rounded (a distance of 2^-40 from an edge) or the point is off the mesh. That is: today's value is the principal value plus the rounded own-triangle term, which is exactly 0 mathematically.

Findings:
1. On a closed mesh today returns values that are neither integers nor the mean of the two sides (1.5 or 2 on a diagonal or an interface of two glued bodies, 0.75 + 0.5 at a reflex edge). The reproducer of the design note (values 5e-15, 1.0, 0.99999, 5e-5, 0.026 at five distances from an edge) is the same defect: the rounded determinant and the cancelling denominator of `TriSolidAngle` at the branch cut.
2. A is exact (an integer on a closed mesh, also on edges, vertices, non-manifold edges and glued interfaces), independent of triangle order (in the probe, on the fixtures of the table; the principal value of B on creases is independent of the order bit for bit too since the canonical order of the summands, see the design note) and 2^k scale, and needs no detection of "on the surface". The side it picks is a function of the coordinate axes (which of y, z, x decides first): for a face with outward normal n it is the side sign(n.y), then sign(n.z), then sign(n.x). A cube face +x has value 0 and -x value 1; rotated by 90 degrees about z the same face has the other side. This is the first paper's definition and the one the crossing count already uses; the consequence for a user is that the value ON the surface is one of the two sides, chosen by the coordinate frame (rotating the mesh can change the side; scaling by 2^k, reordering and translating by dyadic amounts cannot).
3. B is the symmetric alternative (0.5 at a face): for a decision `> 0.5` a face of a closed mesh is a tie (Outside). It needs exact detection of "p in the plane of T" per triangle, which today's code does (`Orient3d`), plus an accurate own term in the plane; no source paper defines it.
4. Soups (`|boundary| >= F`, F > 64): `direct_sum` sums `TriSolidAngle` in floating point with no perturbation, so it is the same defect (T interior: 0.4989 on one side; the other side is -0.5011). A needs an exact own-triangle term there.

## 5. Decision: the principal value (B)

The owner decided (2026-11-24) that the value on the mesh is the principal value (B), not the value at the perturbed point (A). Reason: the existing correction already computed (B) by exact predicates, except for one added floating-point term `own = TriSolidAngle(a, b, c, p) / 4 pi`, which is exactly 0 mathematically (p is in the plane of the triangle) and sits at the branch cut of the formula in floating point. The table above shows it: today minus B is 0 or +-0.5 in every row. Removing the term is the smaller change, keeps the documented and pinned corner and edge values (0.125, 0.25), and gives a value that does not depend on the directions of the coordinate axes (a rotated mesh has the same value on its surface; under (A) a face of a closed mesh is 0 or 1 by which of y, z, x decides the side).

Two steps in `spatial/ExactWindingCache.cs`:
1. The `own` term is removed. The correction subtracts the perturbed value w_T(p_eps) of every triangle that contains p (exactly +-0.5 strictly inside; chi plus the three edge angles on an edge or at a vertex) and adds nothing back. The soup path (`direct_sum`) skips a triangle whose plane contains p (bounding box, then exact `Orient3d`).
2. The triangles that contain p on an edge or at a vertex are collected and resolved by plane: where the sectors of a coplanar group cover every direction the same number m of times (an edge or a vertex inside a flat face), the group's sum is exactly m times +-0.5 by exact predicates, so a flat face of a closed mesh is exactly n + 0.5 also on its inner edges and vertices, however many planes meet at p. Every other group takes the rounded sum. The groups are formed by the exact direction of the normal (all triangles contain p, so equal directions are one plane), no cap on their number, O(n log n); the triangles are summed in a canonical order (each rotated to start at its smallest corner, sorted by the rotated corners), so the value on the surface does not depend on the order of the triangles or their corners, bit for bit, on creases and corners too, and not on the power-of-two scale (review of #1546: the corner of a box differed at 2^-1040 before; a flat face with 10 planes through the point gave 0.5 +- 1 ulp before).

What a threshold decision gives on the surface: 0.5 on a flat face of a closed mesh, so `w > 0.5` is false there, deterministically (Outside), and `w >= 0.5` counts face points as inside; a reflex edge (0.75) and a reflex corner are above 0.5 (Inside); convex edges (0.25), convex corners (0.125) and open boundary edges and vertices are below.

Alternative (A), the value at the perturbed point (the first paper's definition, `chi + boundary sum` without the correction): exact and an integer on closed meshes, independent of order and scale, no detection needed. Not chosen: the side is a function of the coordinate axes (a face with outward normal n takes the side sign(n.y), then sign(n.z), then sign(n.x): cube face +x is 0 and -x is 1; a rotated mesh changes sides), it changes the pinned corner and edge values (0.125 becomes 0 or 1), the soup path would need its own exact term, and the legacy `MeshBoolean` (`MeshMeshCut.RemoveContained`) would read 0 on one of two coincident faces and 1 on the other, so touching inputs would open ("shared face" is no longer closed).

Consequences measured: seven tests moved under (A) in Stage 1; under (B) the pinned values of the cube corner and the bowtie vertex stay, and six tests move (`ExactWindingNumberTests.OnSurfacePoints_...` reference, the two single-triangle tests of `ExactWindingCacheInternalTestsNearOpenEdge` (+-0.5 becomes 0), `FastWindingScaleTests.FastWindingNumber_Invariant("utm")`, and two cases of `MeshMeshCutContactTests.MeshBoolean_OnTouchingCubes_KeepsItsBehaviour`). The legacy `MeshBoolean` on the two touching cases "inner cube on the faces" and "coplanar faces overlap" changes: 15 vertices and 20 triangles become 24 and 36, 31 and 48 become 36 and 60; both results were open before and are open now (8 and 14 boundary edges before, 6 and 12 now), with the volume 0.75 and 1.5 before, 1.25 and 1.75 now, against 1.0 and 1.625 for the exact union: equally wrong in the other direction. "shared face" and "overlap" are unchanged (closed, volumes 2 and 1.875).

Sources: Martens, Trettner, Bessmeltsev, The Antipodal Method (2026), supplement 2.1 (evaluation at the symbolically perturbed point; "winding numbers are not defined on the surface"); Xie, Hafner, Wojtan, Fast and Exact Winding Numbers (2026), section 5 (near-surface values "may vary depending on implementation").
