# Exact 3D Boolean Next-Phase Strategy & Conceptual Reality Check (2026-11-28)

Design and research synthesis across six candidate innovation areas for `ExactMeshBoolean`, `SolidBooleanPipeline`, and `g3cli` / MCP, evaluated on `master` (2026-11-28 Berlin, post-`#1744` `RegionClipping` default-on and post-`#1720` rounding separating axes). No library code was changed; this note records the conceptual audits, prior-art checks, rejected/parked promises, pre-implementation spikes, and the resulting three-phase execution order.

## 2. Prior Art Snapshot ("How Others Solve It")

- **Certifying / Verification (Area 1):** No mainstream 3D mesh Boolean kernel (CGAL, Geogram, Manifold, Cherchi et al., EMBER/Solidean) ships an independently checkable rounded-output proof. Practical reliability comes from exact intermediate invariants (`ExactQuantities`) + fast geometric sanity guards (`NoInventedSurface`, `RoundingVolume`, `TopologyPreservation`).
- **Provenance & Multi-Material (Area 2):** Manifold (Apache-2.0) carries `originalID` + per-vertex property channels via barycentric interpolation; CGAL PMP propagates face indices through corefinement; Zhou et al. (SIGGRAPH 2016) and Da et al. / Wojtan model multi-material complexes on non-manifold arrangements (without double-precision rounding guarantees at triple junctions).
- **Differentiable CSG (Area 3):** Academic differentiable CSG relies on smooth SDF approximations (DiffCSG 2024, UCSG-Net) or frozen-tessellation B-rep AD (`vcad`, Nov 2026) that misses moving intersection topology. Exact Hadamard shape derivatives on double-precision mesh arrangements (building on [EXACT_BOOLEAN_GRADIENTS_2026-11.md](EXACT_BOOLEAN_GRADIENTS_2026-11.md) and Edelsbrunner & Koehl 2003) are unique to `geometry3Sharp`.
- **Scaling Cliffs (Area 4):** Zhou et al. (2016) and Geogram/Lévy use radial-order cell propagation for closed operands; Manifold's `BatchBoolean` uses a balanced merge tree; EMBER (Trettner et al. 2022) uses octree local arrangements with early-out WNTV culling (requiring 26-bit integer quantization, shelved in [PLANE_BASED_KERNEL_STUDY_2026-11.md](PLANE_BASED_KERNEL_STUDY_2026-11.md)); Guo & Fu (SIGGRAPH Asia 2024) group coplanar clusters prior to 2D CDT (comparable to our `#1744`).
- **Agent Reliability & MCP (Area 5):** Recent 2026 systems (CADIR arXiv:2608.00891, `dsh-solidworks`/SolidPilot, AutoCAD-MCP, Axiomize MCP) converge on fail-closed transactions, typed refusal codes, independent deterministic verification gates, and explicit opt-in escalations, backed by evidence (Yu et al., arXiv:2605.27157) that LLMs ignore soft advisory warnings unless blocked hard.
- **Exact `MeshKernel` (Area 6):** Nehring-Wirxel, Kern, Trettner, Kobbelt (EG/SGP 2025, paper only) combine walk-and-zipper half-space cutting with embedded 3D Seidel LP and an AABB proxy (15.5 ms avg vs. CGAL exact 1,830 ms with 314 crashes on 2,762 models).

## 4. Recommended Three-Phase Execution Sequence (Post-Spikes)

- **Phase 1 (Tier S/M, zero exact-kernel risk, highest production ROI):**
  1. `4B-0` (`flat_edge` `same_plane` fallback)
  2. `1A + 1B` (`SolidBooleanPipeline.Verification` v2 + internal certificate hygiene)
  3. `5A–5C` (`SolidBooleanPipeline.Diagnose()` + `SolidBooleanRemediation` + `g3cli`/MCP `solid_boolean` & `sheet_extend`)
  4. `2A` (Opt-in Attribute & Provenance Propagation)
- **Phase 2 (Tier M, unique technical differentiation — both spikes passed):**
  5. `3` (Exact Differentiable Volumetric CSG: $V, M_1, M_2, SE(3)$ + untouched-tri fast path + `SkipMeshOutput`)
  6. `4A-2` (`SolidBooleanPipeline.UnionMany` with `Sphere ∧ PCA-OBB >= 11.0`; `4C-1` dropped by `4C-0`)
- **Phase 3 (Tier L, Spike `2B-0` FULL GO — ready for formal design spec):**
  7. Unified `4A-1` (Patch-Level Cell-Graph DFS + Z3-proved degree-4 `Orient3d` fast path) + `2B` (`ExactMeshPartition` with Policy R2 coupled seam moves + `shared=3` collapse/refusal)
